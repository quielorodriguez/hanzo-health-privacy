## Summary

Hanzo Health is a private, on-device health journaling app. Your health data stays on your device — or in your own encrypted iCloud account if you enable sync. We do not run servers that receive your health information. We do not sell, rent, or share your health data with anyone.

---

## Section 1 — Information Collected and How It Is Used

### 1.1 Apple Health Data (HealthKit)

Hanzo Health requests read-only access to the following Apple Health data types, **if you grant permission** in the iOS Health permissions dialog:

- Sleep analysis
- Step count
- Heart rate (average, resting, and walking average)
- Heart rate variability (HRV SDNN)
- Oxygen saturation (SpO2)
- Respiratory rate
- Active energy burned (calories)
- Basal (resting) energy burned
- Exercise time and stand time
- Workout type and duration
- Menstrual flow
- Body weight
- Body mass index (BMI)
- Blood pressure (systolic and diastolic)
- Blood glucose
- Body temperature
- VO2 max
- Mindful session minutes
- Dietary energy consumed

**Where this data goes:** All values are read directly from Apple Health and stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, they are stored in your private encrypted iCloud account. Hanzo Health never transmits any of these values to our servers or to any third party.

**Permission is yours to control:** You can review and revoke any HealthKit permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Hanzo Health.

### 1.2 Journal and Health Log Data

When you use the journaling features, the following is stored on your device:

- Daily mood, energy level, and symptom ratings you enter
- Free-text notes and entries you write
- Flare log entries (symptom severity and type)
- Condition tags and health categories you select
- Any custom fields you configure

### 1.3 Medication Tracking Data

If you use the medication tracking feature, Hanzo Health stores:

- The names and doses of medications and supplements you enter
- Daily logs of whether each medication was taken and at what time
- Notes you attach to medication logs

This information is stored locally on your device and, if iCloud sync is enabled, in your private encrypted iCloud account. Medication names and adherence logs are never shared with third parties or transmitted to Hanzo's servers.

### 1.4 AI Conversation History

All AI processing happens entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework. Your questions and health data are never sent to Apple's AI servers or to any cloud AI service.

Your AI conversation history is saved on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled, this conversation history is also stored in your private encrypted iCloud account. Conversation history is never sent to Hanzo's servers.

You can delete your conversation history at any time in **Settings → Data → Delete All Data**.

### 1.5 AI-Generated Health Insights

Periodic AI health summaries based on your journal and health data are stored on your device and, if iCloud sync is enabled, in your encrypted iCloud account. They are not transmitted to Hanzo or any third party.

### 1.6 Article Reading History

Records of which health articles you have read are stored on your device and in iCloud (if sync is enabled). This is used only to personalise your article feed.

### 1.7 App Lock and Security Data

If you enable the app lock feature, your chosen 6-digit passcode is stored in your device's secure iOS Keychain. Your passcode never leaves your device and is not shared with Hanzo or any third party.

### 1.8 Biometric Status

The app may read the availability of Face ID or Touch ID to determine whether biometric authentication is available. No biometric data is ever collected, stored, or transmitted.

## Section 2 — On-Device AI and Foundation Models

All AI features operate entirely on your device using Apple Foundation Models. Your health questions and journal data are never sent to Apple's AI servers or to any third-party AI provider.

---

## Section 3 — Data Sync and Storage

### 3.1 Local Storage (Default)

All Hanzo Health data is stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData encrypted data storage.

### 3.2 iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you are signed into iCloud, Hanzo Health can optionally sync your data across your Apple devices using CloudKit. Data synced to iCloud includes journal entries, flare logs, medication records, AI summaries, AI conversation history, and article reading history. All data stored in iCloud is end-to-end encrypted by Apple.

## Section 4 — Third-Party Services

None of the following services receive your health journal content, HealthKit data, medication records, or AI conversations.

### 4.1 RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

We use RevenueCat to manage subscription billing. RevenueCat may receive: a randomly generated app-vendor identifier (IDFV), your IP address, app and iOS version, and purchase event signals from Apple's StoreKit. RevenueCat does not receive any health data. RevenueCat is a US-based service; your purchase data is transferred to the United States under RevenueCat's standard data processing terms. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

**PIPEDA cross-border note:** RevenueCat's processing of purchase data in the United States is subject to US law. You may direct questions about this cross-border transfer to our Privacy Officer.

### 4.2 Apple (HealthKit, iCloud, MetricKit, StoreKit)

- **HealthKit:** Health data is stored locally or in your iCloud — not in Hanzo's systems
- **iCloud CloudKit:** Your encrypted data is managed by Apple under Apple's iCloud Terms
- **MetricKit:** Apple may deliver anonymised crash reports and performance diagnostics (OS version, app version, CPU/memory telemetry) to help us identify crashes. This data does not include your health or journal data. MetricKit diagnostics are retained for up to 90 days, then deleted
- **StoreKit:** Apple processes all in-app purchases. We do not receive your payment information

## Section 5 — Data Retention

We do not operate servers that hold your health data — you control it entirely through your device and iCloud account.

- **Local data:** Retained until you delete it in-app or uninstall the app
- **iCloud data:** Retained in your iCloud account until you delete it in-app or remove Hanzo Health's iCloud access via your Apple ID settings
- **RevenueCat purchase records:** Retained as required for subscription management and legal compliance (typically 7 years for financial records under Canadian law)
- **MetricKit diagnostics:** Retained for up to 90 days for crash analysis, then deleted

## Section 10 — Security and Breach Notification

Your data is protected at multiple levels:

- **On-device storage:** Encrypted by iOS using your device passcode
- **App lock:** Optional 6-digit passcode stored in iOS Secure Keychain
- **iCloud sync:** End-to-end encrypted by Apple; Hanzo cannot access your iCloud data
- **HealthKit access:** Read-only; the app cannot write unsolicited data to Apple Health

**Breach notification — PIPEDA BSSR:** In the event of a breach of security safeguards involving personal information that poses a real risk of significant harm (RROSH) to individuals, we will:

- Report the breach to the **Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada** as soon as feasible
- Notify affected individuals directly if the breach poses a real risk of significant harm
- Maintain a record of all security breaches (including those that do not meet the RROSH threshold) for a minimum of **24 months**

**Additional notifications (where applicable):**

- **Quebec:** Notify the Commission d'accès à l'information within 72 hours of becoming aware of a confidentiality incident posing a risk of serious injury
- **EU/EEA:** 72 hours under GDPR
- **Australia:** Within 30 days under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme
- **New Zealand:** Without undue delay under the Privacy Act 2020

To report a security concern: quielo@hanzotechnologies.com
