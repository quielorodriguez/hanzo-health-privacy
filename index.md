## Our Commitments

- **100% On-Device AI** — Powered by Apple Foundation Models. AI summaries and chat run entirely on your device. No prompts or responses are sent to any server.
- **No Hanzo Servers** — Your journal entries, symptoms, Apple Health data, medications, and AI conversations are never transmitted to Hanzo Technologies.
- **iCloud Sync (Optional)** — If you enable it, your data syncs privately through your own encrypted iCloud account. Hanzo has no access to it.
- **Full Control** — Export, delete, or lock your data at any time from within the app.
- **HealthKit Integration** — Only with your explicit permission. You choose exactly what Apple Health data to share.
