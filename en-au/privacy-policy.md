## Section 2 — The Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)

Hanzo Technologies is bound by the **13 Australian Privacy Principles** under Schedule 1 of the Privacy Act 1988. This policy describes how we comply with each relevant APP.

---

## Section 3 — Personal Information We Collect

### 3.1 Health Information (Sensitive Information)

Health information is **sensitive information** under the Privacy Act (s. 6) and is afforded the highest level of protection. We collect health information only with your **express consent** (APP 3.3(a)).

Health information collected from Apple Health (HealthKit) — **only if you grant permission**:

- Sleep analysis
- Step count
- Heart rate (average, resting, walking average)
- Heart rate variability (HRV SDNN)
- Blood oxygen saturation (SpO2)
- Respiratory rate
- Active and basal energy burned
- Exercise time and stand time
- Workout type and duration
- Menstrual flow
- Body weight and BMI
- Blood pressure (systolic and diastolic)
- Blood glucose
- Body temperature
- VO2 max
- Mindful session minutes
- Dietary energy

Health information you enter directly:
- Daily mood, energy, and symptom ratings
- Free-text journal entries and notes
- Flare logs (symptom severity and type)
- Condition tags and health categories

Medication data (if you use medication tracking):
- Medication names, doses
- Daily adherence logs and notes

### 3.2 Other Personal Information

| Category | Content | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| AI conversation history | AI chat inputs and outputs | Providing AI health insights |
| Purchase / subscription data | Device identifier (IDFV), IP address, purchase events (via RevenueCat) | Subscription management |
| Crash diagnostics | Anonymised crash reports and performance data (via MetricKit) | App stability and improvement |

## Section 4 — How We Collect Personal Information (APP 3)

We collect personal information:
- **Directly from you** when you enter data into the App
- **From Apple HealthKit** when you grant permission for health data access
- **From Apple MetricKit** automatically (anonymised, aggregated crash data only)
- **From RevenueCat** when you purchase a subscription

We do not collect personal information from third parties or public sources.

## Section 5 — How We Use Personal Information (APP 6)

We use personal information only for the primary purpose for which it was collected — to provide the App's health journaling and wellness features to you. We do not use health information for any secondary purpose without your express consent.

## Section 7 — Cross-Border Disclosure (APP 8)

Before disclosing personal information to RevenueCat (United States), we have taken reasonable steps to ensure that RevenueCat does not breach the APPs in relation to that information (APP 8.1). RevenueCat maintains contractual commitments to handle personal information in a manner consistent with the APPs. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

**Canada:** Hanzo Technologies is based in Canada. Australia does not have a formal adequacy list, but Canada's PIPEDA provides a broadly comparable level of protection to the Privacy Act. We take reasonable steps to ensure that your personal information handled in Canada is protected to an equivalent standard.

Your health information, journal data, medication records, and AI conversations are **never disclosed overseas** — they remain on your device or in your personal iCloud account.

All AI features operate entirely on your device using Apple Foundation Models. No health data is sent to external AI systems. AI-generated content is clearly labelled within the App.

## Section 9 — Data Quality and Accuracy (APP 10)

We take reasonable steps to ensure that the personal information we hold about you is accurate, up to date, complete, and relevant. You can update most information directly within the App at any time.

---

## Section 10 — Data Security (APP 11)

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and from unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure:

- **On-device storage:** Protected by iOS device encryption
- **App lock:** Optional 6-digit passcode stored in iOS Secure Keychain
- **iCloud sync:** End-to-end encrypted by Apple; Hanzo cannot access your iCloud data
- **HealthKit access:** Read-only; we cannot write unsolicited data to Apple Health

When personal information is no longer needed for any purpose, we take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify it (APP 11.2).

## Section 11 — Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) Scheme

In the event of an eligible data breach — one that is likely to result in serious harm to individuals — we will:

1. Assess the breach as soon as practicable
2. Notify the **Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)** as soon as practicable after becoming aware
3. Notify affected individuals directly, or publish a statement on our website if direct notification is not practicable

**Timeframe:** We aim to notify the OAIC and affected individuals within **30 days** of becoming aware of an eligible breach, or sooner where practicable.

---

## Section 12 — Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024

The **Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024** introduced significant changes to the Privacy Act. Key provisions relevant to Hanzo Health:

- **Statutory tort for serious invasions of privacy:** A new cause of action allows individuals to sue for serious invasions of privacy. We are committed to handling your personal information in a way that respects your reasonable expectation of privacy.
- **Children's Online Privacy Code:** A children's privacy code is being developed. Once enacted, we will comply with its requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 15.
- **Automated decision-making:** Where AI-generated insights might have a significant effect on you, we ensure transparency. All Hanzo Health AI outputs are clearly labelled and are intended for personal wellness use only — not clinical decisions.
- **Direct right of action:** Individuals may now seek compensation for serious invasions of privacy directly. Our data handling practices are designed to avoid any such breach.
