## Notice to UK Residents

This UK Privacy Policy supplements and, for UK residents, supersedes our global base Privacy Policy. It is provided pursuant to the **UK General Data Protection Regulation** (UK GDPR, as retained in UK law by the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018) and the **Data Protection Act 2018** (DPA 2018).

UK GDPR diverges from EU GDPR following Brexit. This policy reflects UK-specific requirements, including ICO authority, UK data transfer mechanisms, and UK data subject rights.

## Summary

Hanzo Health is a private, on-device health journaling app. Your health data stays on your device or in your own encrypted iCloud account. We do not run servers that receive your health information. We do not sell, rent, or share your health data with anyone.

## Section 2 — Data We Process and Legal Basis

Health data is a **special category of personal data** under Article 9 UK GDPR and receives the highest level of protection.

| Data Category | Examples | Legal Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Health data (HealthKit)** | Sleep, heart rate, menstrual cycle, blood glucose, weight, VO2 max, and 15 other types | **Explicit consent** — Article 9(2)(a) UK GDPR |
| **Journal and symptom data** | Mood ratings, free-text notes, flare logs, condition tags | **Explicit consent** — Article 9(2)(a) UK GDPR |
| **Medication tracking data** | Medication names, doses, adherence logs | **Explicit consent** — Article 9(2)(a) UK GDPR |
| **AI conversation history** | Messages you send to the AI assistant; AI responses | **Explicit consent** — Article 9(2)(a) UK GDPR |
| **Subscription / purchase data** | App-vendor device identifier (IDFV), IP address, purchase events | **Contract performance** — Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR |
| **Crash diagnostics (MetricKit)** | Anonymised crash reports, CPU/memory telemetry, app version | **Legitimate interests** — Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR |

**Withdrawing consent:** You may withdraw consent at any time by revoking HealthKit permissions (iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Hanzo Health) or by deleting your data (Settings → Data → Delete All Data). Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

## Section 3 — Where Your Data Is Stored

All health data, journal entries, medication records, and AI conversation history are stored **on your device** using Apple's SwiftData encrypted storage. None of this data is transmitted to Hanzo's servers.

If you enable iCloud sync, your data is stored in your **private encrypted iCloud account** managed by Apple, subject to Apple's iCloud Terms and Privacy Policy.

---

## Section 4 — Third-Party Processors

None of the following processors receive your health data, journal content, medication records, or AI conversations.

### 4.1 RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

**Purpose:** Subscription billing and entitlement management  
**Data transferred:** App-vendor device identifier (IDFV), IP address, app version, iOS version, purchase event signals  
**Location:** United States  
**Transfer mechanism:** UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) issued by the ICO, or UK Addendum to EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)  
**Privacy policy:** https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

### 4.2 Apple Inc. (HealthKit, iCloud CloudKit, MetricKit, StoreKit)

**HealthKit:** Health data stored locally or in your iCloud. Not transmitted to Hanzo.  
**iCloud CloudKit:** Encrypted data in your personal iCloud account. Governed by Apple's iCloud Terms and Apple's UK data transfer agreements.  
**MetricKit:** Anonymised crash and performance diagnostics. No health or journal data included. Retained up to 90 days.  
**StoreKit:** Apple processes all in-app purchases. We do not receive payment data.

## Section 5 — International Data Transfers

Hanzo Technologies Inc. is based in Canada. The UK has adopted an **adequacy regulation** for Canada under UK GDPR Article 45 (The Adequacy Decisions for Canada (Commercial Organisations) Regulations), meaning transfers of personal data from the UK to Hanzo Technologies are permitted without additional safeguards.

For transfers to RevenueCat (United States), we rely on the **UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA)** issued by the ICO, or the **UK Addendum** to EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Copies are available on request: quielo@hanzotechnologies.com.

## Section 6 — AI Features

All AI features in Hanzo Health operate entirely on your device using Apple Foundation Models. No health data is sent to external AI systems. AI-generated content (Hanzo Score, insights, summaries) is clearly labelled within the App.

## Section 7 — Data Retention

- **On-device data:** Retained until you delete it in-app or uninstall the app
- **iCloud data:** Retained in your iCloud account until you delete it or revoke iCloud access
- **Purchase data (RevenueCat):** Retained for the subscription period plus 7 years for financial compliance

## Section 8 — Your Rights Under UK GDPR

As a UK resident you have the following rights:

| Right | What It Means | How to Exercise |
|---|---|---|
| **Access (Art. 15)** | Right to a copy of your personal data | Contact us or use in-app export |
| **Rectification (Art. 16)** | Right to correct inaccurate data | Edit directly in the app |
| **Erasure (Art. 17)** | Right to have your data deleted | Settings → Data → Delete All Data, or contact us |
| **Restriction (Art. 18)** | Right to restrict processing in certain circumstances | Contact us |
| **Portability (Art. 20)** | Right to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format | Settings → Data → Export; other formats on request |
| **Objection (Art. 21)** | Right to object to processing based on legitimate interests | Contact us |
| **Withdraw consent** | Right to withdraw consent at any time | Revoke HealthKit permissions or delete all data |
| **Lodge a complaint** | Right to complain to the ICO | See Section 9 |

**Response times:** We will acknowledge your request within 5 business days and respond substantively within **one calendar month** (extendable by two further months for complex requests, with notice).

## Section 10 — Children

Hanzo Health is intended for users aged **13 and over** in the UK. Consistent with the UK Children's Code (Age Appropriate Design Code), we apply the following protections for users under 18:

- We do not use health data for profiling or targeted advertising
- We do not share children's data with third parties for their own purposes
- We apply data minimisation principles

If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, please contact quielo@hanzotechnologies.com and we will delete the relevant data promptly.

## Section 11 — Security and Breach Notification

Your data is protected by iOS device encryption, optional Keychain app lock, and Apple end-to-end iCloud encryption.

**UK GDPR breach notification (Articles 33–34):** In the event of a personal data breach, we will:
- Notify the ICO within **72 hours** of becoming aware, where the breach is likely to result in a risk to individuals' rights and freedoms
- Notify affected individuals **without undue delay** if the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms
