## Section 2 — Description of Service

Hanzo Health is a personal health journaling application for iOS that allows you to:

- Track daily mood, energy, symptoms, and health metrics
- Log medications and adherence
- View health data from Apple Health (HealthKit)
- Receive AI-generated health insights using Apple's on-device Foundation Models

Hanzo Health is a **wellness and journaling tool only**. It is not a medical device under the Medicines Act 1981 (NZ) or the Medical Devices Regulations. It does not provide medical diagnoses and is not a substitute for professional health advice. Always consult a qualified health professional.
