## Section 2 — The Information Privacy Principles (IPPs)

Hanzo Technologies is bound by the **13 Information Privacy Principles** set out in the Privacy Act 2020. The following sections describe how we comply with each relevant principle.

---

## Section 3 — Personal Information We Collect (IPP 1 and IPP 3)

We collect only the personal information necessary to provide the App's features to you (IPP 1 — purpose of collection). We collect personal information directly from you wherever reasonably practicable (IPP 2).

### 3.1 Health Information (Sensitive by Nature)

While the Privacy Act 2020 does not define a separate category of "sensitive" information, health information is recognised as information that can be particularly sensitive and is collected only with your **express consent**.

Health information from Apple Health (HealthKit) — only with your permission:
- Sleep analysis, step count, heart rate, HRV, SpO2, respiratory rate
- Active and basal energy, exercise and stand time, workout data
- Menstrual flow, body weight, BMI, blood pressure, blood glucose, body temperature, VO2 max, mindfulness minutes, dietary energy

Health information you enter directly:
- Mood, energy, and symptom ratings
- Free-text journal notes and entries
- Flare logs, condition tags, custom fields

Medication tracking data (if used):
- Medication names, doses, and daily adherence logs

### 3.2 Other Personal Information

| Category | Content | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| AI conversation history | AI chat inputs and outputs | AI health insights feature |
| Purchase data (RevenueCat) | Device identifier (IDFV), IP address, purchase events | Subscription management |
| Crash diagnostics (MetricKit) | Anonymised crash reports and performance data | App stability |

## Section 4 — Notice at Collection (IPP 3)

At or before the time we collect personal information from you, we take reasonable steps to ensure you are aware of:

- The fact that the information is being collected and the purpose for collection
- The intended recipients of the information
- The name and address of the agency collecting the information
- Your right to access and correct the information

This Privacy Policy provides that notice.

---

## Section 5 — How We Use Personal Information (IPP 10 and IPP 11)

We use personal information only for the primary purpose for which it was collected. We do not use personal information for secondary purposes without your consent, except as permitted by the Privacy Act 2020.

We do not disclose health information, journal content, or medication records to any third party.

---

## Section 6 — Disclosure Overseas (IPP 12)

We disclose limited non-health personal information to overseas recipients:

| Recipient | Country | Information | Safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|
| RevenueCat, Inc. | United States | Device identifier, IP address, purchase events | Contractual obligations equivalent to IPPs |
| Apple Inc. (MetricKit) | United States | Anonymised crash diagnostics | Apple's Privacy Policy |

Before disclosing personal information to RevenueCat overseas, we have ensured that RevenueCat will not hold the information in a way that, in all the circumstances, breaches the IPPs (IPP 12(1)(b)).

**Canada:** Hanzo Technologies is based in Canada. Canada's privacy laws (PIPEDA) provide a comparable level of protection. We take reasonable steps to ensure your personal information in Canada is protected consistently with the IPPs.

---

## Section 7 — AI Features

All AI features operate entirely on your device using Apple Foundation Models. No health data is sent to external AI systems. AI-generated content is clearly labelled within the App.

---

## Section 8 — Data Security (IPP 5)

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse, and unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure:

- iOS device encryption (on-device storage)
- Optional Keychain-secured 6-digit app lock
- Apple end-to-end encryption (iCloud sync)
- Read-only HealthKit access

## Section 9 — Mandatory Breach Notification

Under the Privacy Act 2020, we are required to notify the **Privacy Commissioner** and affected individuals of any privacy breach that has caused, or is likely to cause, serious harm.

We will:

- Notify affected individuals **as soon as practicable** after notifying the Commissioner (or at the same time)

## Section 10 — Your Rights (IPP 6 and IPP 7)

**Right to access (IPP 6):** You have the right to request access to personal information we hold about you. We will respond within **20 working days** (as required by the Privacy Act 2020).

**Right to correction (IPP 7):** You have the right to request correction of personal information we hold about you that is inaccurate, misleading, or not up to date.

**In-app controls:**
- Delete all data: Settings → Data → Delete All Data
- Export data: Settings → Data → Export (PDF)
- Revoke HealthKit: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Hanzo Health
